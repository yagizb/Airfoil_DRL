This study presents an optimization framework based on Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL),
encompassing policy learning and execution process. For computational fluid dynamics (CFD)
analysis, three fidelity levels are employed: low (Xfoil), medium (CFL3D-RANS) and high (SOD2DLES).
The effectiveness of the established framework is demonstrated through
multi-point optimization of airfoils, targeting the improvements in aerodynamics metrics such as
lift coefficient (𝐶𝐿) and lift-to-drag (L/D).
